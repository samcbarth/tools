Why do these tools run in the browser?

Every tool on Toolbench does its work with JavaScript on your own device, so your files, lists and text never leave your computer.

That makes them safe for client data, contact lists and drafts. It also means they work offline once the page has loaded. The only tools that touch the network are the robots.txt and sitemap checker, which asks the site you name for its public file directly from your browser, and the image tools, which never upload anything.
