URL slug generator

Paste one title or many, one per line, and get clean hyphenated slugs with accents and punctuation removed.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or open here is uploaded to any server.

How do I make a good URL slug?

Use a few lowercase words that describe the page, separated by hyphens, with no accents, punctuation or dates that will go stale. This tool converts any title into that format.

  1. Paste a title, or several on separate lines.
  2. Keep the hyphen separator, which search engines read as a space.
  3. Set a max length if your CMS has a limit.
  4. Turn on stop word removal for shorter slugs.
  5. Copy the results.

Frequently asked questions

Hyphens or underscores?

Hyphens. Google's URL guidance recommends hyphens to separate words, because underscores can join words together.

Should I remove stop words?

It is optional. Removing words like the and of shortens slugs, but keep them if the slug reads badly without them.

Should I change slugs on pages that are already live?

Only with a 301 redirect from the old URL, or you lose links and rankings that point to it.

Does it handle non-English letters?

It strips accents (é to e) and maps a few special letters like ß to ss. Scripts without a Latin form are removed, so check slugs for those pages by hand.
