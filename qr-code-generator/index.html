QR code generator

Type a link or text, or fill in Wi-Fi or email details, and download a QR code as a PNG or a scalable SVG.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or open here is uploaded to any server.

How do I make a QR code for a link?

Paste the full URL, including https://, then download the code as PNG for screens or SVG for print. The codes made here are static: they point straight at your link and never expire.

  1. Choose Link or text, Wi-Fi, or Email.
  2. Enter the details.
  3. Pick error correction; Medium suits most uses, High if you will put a logo over it.
  4. Keep dark modules on a light background for reliable scanning.
  5. Download PNG or SVG and test-scan it before printing.

Examples

Wi-Fi login

Input

Network: CafeGuest
Password: latte2026
Security: WPA

Result

WIFI:T:WPA;S:CafeGuest;P:latte2026;;

Tracking scans

To count scans in analytics, encode a link with UTM tags, for example utm_medium=qr. Build it with the UTM builder first.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. They are static codes that contain your text or link directly, with no redirect service in between.

PNG or SVG?

SVG scales to any size without blur, which is best for print. PNG is easiest for slides, documents and websites.

Can I change the colors?

Yes, but keep strong contrast and a darker foreground than background. Inverted or low-contrast codes fail on some scanners.

Is my Wi-Fi password sent anywhere?

No. The code is drawn in your browser. It is still readable by anyone who scans the printed code, so only share guest network details.
