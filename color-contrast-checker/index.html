Color contrast checker

Pick a text and background color to get the WCAG contrast ratio, pass or fail for each level, and a suggested fix when it fails.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or open here is uploaded to any server.

What contrast ratio do I need for accessible text?

WCAG 2 level AA needs at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24 pixels, or about 18.66 pixels bold). Level AAA needs 7:1 and 4.5:1. Enter your colors to see your ratio.

  1. Enter or pick the text color.
  2. Enter or pick the background color.
  3. Read the ratio and the pass or fail table.
  4. If normal text fails AA, try the suggested color.
  5. Re-check hover, disabled and placeholder states too.

Frequently asked questions

How is the ratio calculated?

With the WCAG 2 formula: (lighter relative luminance + 0.05) divided by (darker + 0.05), using sRGB gamma-corrected luminance.

Does placeholder text need to pass?

WCAG applies contrast requirements to text that conveys information, which includes placeholder text people rely on to understand a field.

What about text on images?

Check against the lightest and darkest parts of the image behind the text, or add a solid or semi-transparent overlay.

Is this the same as WCAG 3 or APCA?

No. This uses the WCAG 2 ratio, which is what current accessibility laws and audits reference.
